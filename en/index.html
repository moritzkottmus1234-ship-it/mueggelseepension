The House

A villa with a rowing history

The villa was built in the late 19th century. In 1925 the academic rowing club “Alt Brandenburg” turned it into its guesthouse. Today you are our guest here, right on Berlin’s Lake Müggelsee.

When we rebuilt the house in 2008 and 2009, we worked from the 1925 plans on purpose. The result is a stylish house with modern comfort that shows its history. Josef-Nawrocki-Straße is a quiet one-way street with no through traffic, so you sleep well even on the street side.

  1. Late 19th c.The villa on today’s Josef-Nawrocki-Straße is built.
  2. 1925Rebuilt as the guesthouse of the academic rowing club “Alt Brandenburg”.
  3. 2008 and 2009Rebuilt again from the 1925 plans, with modern comfort.
  4. Since May 2009The MüggelSeepension welcomes its guests.

Location and area

Water on your doorstep, Berlin in 30 minutes

Friedrichshagen is part of Köpenick and one of Berlin’s favourite places for a day out. Lake Müggelsee, the Müggelspree river, the Spree tunnel and Bölschestraße with its restaurants are right outside the door.

  • FriedrichshagenA walk along the lake, through the Spree tunnel and down Bölschestraße.
  • KöpenickOld town, town hall and the famous Captain of Köpenick.
  • Berlin centreAbout 30 minutes by S-Bahn or car to the Brandenburg Gate and TV Tower.
  • SurroundingsVia the A10 you reach the Spreewald or Tropical Islands quickly.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Your question is not here? Call us: +49 30 640 903 26

Is there a minimum stay?

We do not take bookings for a single night. The minimum stay depends on season and occupancy. Just ask us.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. All room rates include the generous breakfast buffet in the hall facing the river.

Where can I park?

Free parking spaces on the property are available subject to availability. If they are taken, drive into the entrance briefly to unload and then park on the street. In summer and at weekends the walk may be a few steps longer.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, by arrangement. Please mention it in your request. We charge €5 per night for pets.

Is there a step-free room?

Yes. On the ground floor we have a step-free double room for guests for whom stairs are an obstacle.

What is the City Tax?

Berlin’s City Tax is an accommodation tax charged by the city. It is added to the room rate and also applies to business stays.

Can I cancel for free?

Yes, up to 2 weeks before your arrival. After that, staggered fees apply, listed under rates.
